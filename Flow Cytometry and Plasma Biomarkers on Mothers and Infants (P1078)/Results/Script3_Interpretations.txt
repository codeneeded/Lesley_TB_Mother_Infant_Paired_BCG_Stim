================================================================================
SCRIPT 3 — INTERPRETATION SUMMARY
Flow Cytometry and Plasma Biomarkers on Mothers and Infants (P1078)
Plasma biomarker analyses
================================================================================

Four comparisons, analogous in design to Script 2's Comp1-Comp4 but applied
to 34 plasma analytes (after low-information filtering) instead of cellular
flow readouts.

  P1   Infants_12wk_vs_44wk_Paired
       Paired Wilcoxon within each infant: does the analyte change from
       3 months to ~10 months of age?

  P2   Infants_byMaternalIGRA_PosVsNeg
       Unpaired Wilcoxon: do infants of IGRA-Pos mothers have different
       plasma levels than infants of IGRA-Neg mothers?

  P3   Infants_byInfantIGRA_PosVsNeg
       Unpaired Wilcoxon: do IGRA-Pos infants differ from IGRA-Neg infants?

  P4   Mothers_Entry_byMaternalIGRA_PosVsNeg
       Unpaired Wilcoxon at Entry: do IGRA-Pos mothers differ from
       IGRA-Neg mothers in their systemic plasma profile?

Statistical tests run on log1p-transformed values; identical p-values would
result from running on z-scored values because the test is rank-based.
P-values are BH-adjusted within each comparison.

================================================================================

  ----                                            ----------  -------  ----------
  P1  Infants 12wk -> 44wk, paired                       34         4           9

================================================================================
P1 — INFANT MATURATION 12wk -> 44wk (PAIRED)
File: Plasma_StatTests/Infants_12wk_vs_44wk_Paired/stats_Infants_12vs44_paired.csv
================================================================================

Four analytes change significantly between 3 and ~10 months of age
(FDR q < 0.05):

  Analyte    n   Δ log1p   Direction   p_adj
  -------    -   -------   ---------   -----
  CD14      31    +0.28    Up at 44w   9.1e-05
  TNF-RI    18    -0.28    Down        0.016
  IL-22     31    +0.97*   Up at 44w   0.024
  TNF-RII   16    -0.15    Down        0.041

  * IL-22: median at 12w = 0 (below LLOQ in most infants), median at 44w
    = 0.97. The Wilcoxon detects a directional shift even though the
    median of paired differences is 0 (most pairs change from 0 to a
    detectable value, with few going the other way).

Biological reading
------------------
Soluble CD14 (sCD14, a monocyte-shed marker) rises across the first
year. The TNF receptor decoys (TNF-RI, TNF-RII) both decline. IL-22
moves from below detection to detectable in a meaningful subset of
infants.

The pattern is consistent with reduced systemic TNF signaling and
maturation of monocyte-driven innate immunity. The IL-22 emergence
parallels the cellular finding in Script 2 Comp1 (CCR6+ CXCR3- Th17-
like CD4 cells expanding 12w -> 44w under BCG), supporting a coherent
Th17/IL-22 axis maturing in this window.

Direction overall
-----------------
Among the 9 raw p < 0.05 analytes, 6 decrease and 2 increase between 12
and 44 weeks (1 IL-22 is direction-ambiguous due to the zero-floor).
The dominant signal is a reduction in inflammatory plasma markers as
infants mature.

P2 — INFANTS BY MATERNAL IGRA (POS vs NEG)
File: Plasma_StatTests/Infants_byMaternalIGRA_PosVsNeg/
================================================================================

  n_pos median = 16   range [8, 16]   (one row per infant, pooled across
  n_neg median = 13   range [7, 13]    12wk and 44wk where both exist)

Zero FDR-significant analytes; one raw p < 0.05 hit:

  iFABP   Pos median 10.07, Neg 9.70   (+0.37 log1p)   raw p = 0.015,
                                                       p_adj = 0.51

iFABP is a marker of intestinal epithelial damage / gut permeability;
the direction (higher in infants of IGRA-Pos mothers) is biologically
plausible but does not survive FDR with this sample size.

The data are consistent with no detectable systemic plasma signature
of in-utero maternal IGRA exposure in the infant at the available
sample size.

P4 — MOTHERS AT ENTRY BY MATERNAL IGRA (POS vs NEG)
File: Plasma_StatTests/Mothers_Entry_byMaternalIGRA_PosVsNeg/
================================================================================

  n_pos median = 17   range [10, 17]
  n_neg median = 13   range [ 7, 13]

Zero analytes at raw p < 0.05. The minimum raw p across all 34
analytes is 0.071 (IL-6). No FDR-significant findings.

This is a clean negative result and contrasts informatively with
Script 2 Comp4, which found Mtb-specific cellular response differences
between IGRA-Pos and IGRA-Neg mothers under E6C10 (KW raw p < 0.005
for multiple Th1 readouts). Maternal IGRA status manifests in the
T-cell memory compartment (inducible, antigen-specific) but not in
the circulating plasma cytokine/biomarker profile at Entry.


================================================================================
CROSS-CUTTING OBSERVATIONS
================================================================================

Infant plasma matures markedly between 3 and ~10 months
-------------------------------------------------------
Four FDR-significant changes in P1 (CD14 up; TNF-RI, TNF-RII down;
IL-22 emerging) describe coordinated maturation of innate-inflammatory
signaling and Th17-axis emergence. The IL-22 result aligns with the
cellular Th17 expansion seen in Script 2 Comp1.

Maternal IGRA status does not appear in plasma
----------------------------------------------
P4 shows no plasma differences between IGRA-Pos and IGRA-Neg mothers
at Entry, despite Script 2 Comp4 showing clear differences in their
cellular Mtb-specific responses. This dissociation indicates that
the IGRA-status biology is captured by antigen-specific memory T cells
rather than by systemic circulating biomarkers in this cohort.

The maternal IGRA effect does not propagate to infant plasma either
-------------------------------------------------------------------
P2 shows no FDR-significant plasma signature in infants of IGRA-Pos
versus IGRA-Neg mothers, consistent with the negative cross-sectional
result in Script 2 Comp2 for cellular readouts. Together, P2 and the
flow Comp2 support a "no detectable population-level transfer" reading
at this sample size.


================================================================================
IMPLICATIONS FOR SCRIPT 4 (LASSO MODELING)
================================================================================

  The maternal plasma layer in Script 4 uses Entry-timepoint maternal
  plasma to predict infant 12wk BCG response. P4 establishes that
  maternal plasma does not separate IGRA-Pos from IGRA-Neg mothers at
  the population level. Maternal plasma may still predict individual
  infant response — the LASSO may identify combinations of analytes
  carrying signal that no single analyte test detects — but a strong
  univariate predictor was not anticipated and is not present.

  Infant IGRA status as a stratification or prediction axis is not
  supported by this script (n_pos = 5 after per-PID collapse).

  The four analytes with maturation signals (CD14, TNF-RI, TNF-RII,
  IL-22) are infant-timepoint changes and are not part of the maternal-
  feature predictor set in Script 4. They are flagged here for
  potential follow-up as developmental references rather than as
  modeling inputs.


================================================================================
LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

Sample size and missingness
---------------------------
Roughly one-third of analytes (12 of 34) have substantially reduced
paired counts in P1 (n_pairs = 16-18) compared to the rest (n = 31).
This reflects high upstream missingness in those specific assays
(D-Dimer, IL-27, TNF-RII, TNF-RI, CD25, CD163, ICAM-1, IL-18, CRP,
MMP-9, VCAM-1, iFABP — most have ~43 NA cells in the source data).
Power to detect change is correspondingly lower in this subset.

In P3, the IGRA-positive infant subgroup contains only ~5 distinct
individuals after per-PID collapse across timepoints. Negative
findings in P3 are not interpretable as evidence of absence; positive
findings should be treated as hypothesis-generating only.

Pooling across timepoints in P2/P3
----------------------------------
P2 and P3 pool 12wk and 44wk observations for each infant (collapsed
per PID with the median across timepoints). This averages out
timepoint-specific effects and assumes the maternal-IGRA or infant-
IGRA effect is stable across the two timepoints. A timepoint-stratified
analysis would have lower power per stratum but could reveal effects
specific to one developmental window.

UND/below-LLOQ handling and the IL-22 quirk
-------------------------------------------
UND and below-LLOQ values (e.g. "<0.80↓") are recoded to 0 before log1p
transformation. This is appropriate for rank-based testing but produces
visualization artifacts when the median of paired differences is 0 for
an analyte whose Wilcoxon p-value is nonetheless small. IL-22 in P1 is
the clearest example: most infants are at 0 at 12 weeks, a subset are
detectable at 44 weeks, the Wilcoxon detects the asymmetry, but the
median Δ summary statistic reads 0. The p-value is correct; the median
Δ effect size is uninformative for this analyte.

Multiple-testing burden
-----------------------
Each comparison applies BH-FDR within its own analyte panel (34 tests).
This is less aggressive than the 250-300 tests per comparison in
Script 2 but still removes single-analyte raw signals from P2 and P3
that might warrant follow-up in a larger cohort.

Cross-sectional design (P2, P3, P4)
-----------------------------------
P2, P3, and P4 are cross-sectional comparisons. They do not support
causal inference about maternal-to-infant transfer or about progression
from IGRA-Neg to IGRA-Pos status. P1 (paired longitudinal) is the only
design in Script 3 that supports within-individual inference.

IGRA semantics
--------------
Maternal IGRA "INT" values are treated as positive in the IGRA lookup
used by Script 3; "ATB" is treated as a separate clinical category and
is not included in the Pos vs Neg contrast in P4. The ATB stratum is
not analyzed separately in plasma at this stage.
