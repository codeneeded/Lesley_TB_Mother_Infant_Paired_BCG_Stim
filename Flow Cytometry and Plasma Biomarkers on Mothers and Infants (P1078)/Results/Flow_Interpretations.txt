SCRIPT 2 — INTERPRETATION SUMMARY
Flow Cytometry and Plasma Biomarkers on Mothers and Infants (P1078)
================================================================================

Two distinct analyses, two different questions.

  PART A   _ALL_readouts_summary.csv
           Within each PID, does the Proliferating fraction differ from the
           Non-Proliferating fraction in the % of cells expressing each
           readout? Stratified by Group x Condition x Compartment x Readout.

  PART B   Comp1-Comp4 (Flow_ProlifOnly_GroupComparisons/StatTables_CSV/)
           Among ONLY the proliferating cells, do response compositions
           differ across time (Comp1), maternal IGRA (Comp2), infant IGRA
           (Comp3), or maternal disease state (Comp4)?


================================================================================
PART A. PROLIFERATING vs NON-PROLIFERATING (PAIRED WILCOXON)
File: Flow_Prolif_vs_NonProlif_PairedTests/_ALL_readouts_summary.csv
================================================================================

Scale
-----
  588 tests total
  350 (60%) significant at FDR q < 0.05

The high hit rate reflects the analytical design rather than a screening
issue: proliferating and non-proliferating cells are biologically distinct
populations by construction, so most readouts are expected to differ. The
informative result is the direction and magnitude pattern.


Headline pattern: Prolif cells display an activated effector phenotype
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Among 350 significant rows, 287 (82%) show Prolif > NonProlif. The markers
sort along the effector-naive axis:

  UP IN PROLIF (median delta pp, Prolif minus NonProlif)
    TNFa                  +31
    HLA-DR+ CD38+         +30
    CD45RO+ CD27+         +26     (effector memory)
    CD107a                +26     (degranulation)
    CD45RO+ CD27-         +21
    PD-1                  +16
    IL-2                  +15
    IFNy                  +13
    IL-21, IL-22, IL-17a  +5 to +9
    CCR6/CXCR3 subsets    +8 to +12

  DOWN IN PROLIF
    CD45RO+ CD27+ /CXCR5+ -59     (Tfh-like memory)
    CD45RO- CD27+         -27     (naive)
    CCR6- CXCR3-          -21     (Th2-like double-neg)
    CD45RO- CD27-         -13     (terminal effector)

Cells that proliferated after stimulation are enriched for effector
cytokine production, activation markers, and effector-memory
differentiation, while depleted of naive, terminal-effector, and Tfh-like
phenotypes. This is the expected antigen-specific responder signature and
supports the validity of the proliferation gate as a marker of antigen-
specific response.

The CXCR5+ memory subset shows a notably large depletion in Prolif
(especially in mothers at Entry, -60 to -70 pp). Tfh-like cells provide
B-cell help rather than expanding themselves on stimulation, so their
relative absence from the proliferating fraction is biologically
consistent.


Sample sizes by group
---------------------
  Infants 12wks    median n = 34  (max 35)
  Infants 44wks    median n = 32
  Mothers Entry    median n = 24
  Mothers Dx       median n =  5  (range 0-6)


Tie-in to Script 4
------------------
In the Infant 12wk BCG block — the stratum used as the modeling outcome
in Script 4 — 42 readouts are FDR-significant at n = 35, with effect
sizes up to +/-58 pp. The proliferating CD8 compartment is a distinct
cell population from the non-proliferating CD8 compartment, supporting
the use of its delta as the modeling outcome.

PART B. PROLIFERATING-ONLY GROUP COMPARISONS (Comp1-Comp4)
Files: Flow_ProlifOnly_GroupComparisons/StatTables_CSV/Comp{1..4}_*.csv
================================================================================

Summary across all four comparisons
-----------------------------------

  Test                                            n_tests  FDR-sig  Raw p<0.05
  ----                                            -------  -------  ----------
  Comp1  Infants 12wk -> 44wk, paired                100         2          15
  Comp2  Infants, Maternal IGRA Pos vs Neg            300         0          23
  Comp3  Infants, Infant IGRA Pos vs Neg              300         0          41
  Comp4  Mothers KW across Pos/Neg/ATB                250         0          35

Comp1 is the only comparison with FDR-significant findings.


COMP1 — Infant maturation 12wk -> 44wk (paired, n ~ 33)
-------------------------------------------------------

Two FDR-significant findings, both CD4 Th-polarization shifts under BCG:

  CCR6- CXCR3+ (Th1-like) CD4    33% -> 24%    q = 0.011    n = 34
  CCR6+ CXCR3- (Th17-like) CD4    4.6% -> 8.1%  q = 0.038    n = 34

A Th1-skewed proliferating response at 3 months gives way to a broader
Th1/Th17 mix by ~10 months.

Among the 15 raw-p hits, 11 of 15 are upregulated at 44 wks (TNFa, IL-21,
IL-22, IFNy, HLA-DR+CD38+, CD107a). The proliferating compartment
acquires more effector function with age, consistent with normal post-
neonatal immune maturation.


COMP2 — Maternal IGRA effect on infants (unpaired, n_pos ~ 18, n_neg ~ 15)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Zero FDR-significant, 23 raw-p hits with balanced direction (13 up in
Pos, 10 down). The strongest raw signals do not form a coherent pattern
across stimulations or timepoints.

The data are consistent with no detectable population-level effect of
in-utero maternal IGRA exposure on the composition of the infant
proliferating-cell compartment at 12 or 44 weeks at this sample size.


COMP3 — Infant's own IGRA status (unpaired, n_pos ~ 9, n_neg ~ 25)
------------------------------------------------------------------

Zero FDR-significant, 41 raw-p hits — the most of any comparison, but
also the most underpowered. Direction is skewed: 29 of 41 raw hits show
Pos < Neg.

Two patterns reproduce across conditions:

  CD107a (degranulation) CD8 / BCG / 44wks
    Pos -24 pp vs Neg, Cliff's delta = -0.79, raw p = 0.0006

  CD45RO+ CD27+ memory CD4
    Approximately -30 pp in Pos at 44 wks across MED and DosR


COMP4 — Maternal IGRA Pos/Neg/ATB (Kruskal-Wallis, n median ~ 35)
-----------------------------------------------------------------

Zero FDR-significant, 35 raw-p KW hits. The pattern is the most
biologically coherent of the cross-sectional comparisons.

  Th1 polarization (CCR6- CXCR3+) in CD4 separates IGRA status across
  multiple stimulations:
    BCG     KW raw p = 0.0003
    DosR    KW raw p = 0.001
    E6C10   KW raw p = 0.001

  HLA-DR+CD38+ activation and CD45RO+CD27+ memory in CD8/gdTCR
  under E6C10:  p = 0.002 - 0.008

Pairwise post-hoc: 18 of 36 pairwise hits are Pos vs Neg, and 12 of those
18 are under E6C10 specifically.

E6C10 is the Mtb-specific antigen, so the Pos vs Neg difference under
E6C10 is biologically expected: IGRA-positive women (Mtb-sensitized)
respond to Mtb antigens; IGRA-negative women do not. The cross-reactive
BCG signal is also present; DosR and GAG less clearly so. This fits an
antigen-specificity gradient.


================================================================================
IMPLICATIONS FOR SCRIPT 4 (LASSO MODELING)
================================================================================

  Comp4 indicates that maternal IGRA-Pos and IGRA-Neg mothers have
  detectably different proliferating-cell composition under stimulation,
  particularly under the Mtb-specific antigen E6C10.

  Comp2 indicates that this maternal difference is not visible at the
  population level in infants. Maternal features may still predict
  infant binary response in Script 4, but a large population-level
  signal is not anticipated.

  The IGRA-positive infant subgroup is too small (n = 9) to support
  reliable inference involving infant IGRA status as a predictor or
  stratification axis.


================================================================================
LIMITATIONS
================================================================================

Sample size and statistical power
---------------------------------
The Mothers Dx stratum has a median of 5 paired observations (range
0-6). Only 3 of 196 tests in this group reach raw significance, and
negative findings in this stratum cannot be interpreted as evidence of
absence.

The IGRA-positive infant subgroup contains 9 individuals against 25
IGRA-negative infants (Comp3). Effect estimates in this comparison are
unstable, and the direction skew (29 of 41 raw hits showing Pos < Neg)
is consistent with both real biology and statistical artifact.
Replication in a larger IGRA-positive infant cohort would be required
to disambiguate.

Multiple-testing burden
-----------------------
Comp2, Comp3, and Comp4 yield no FDR-significant findings despite
biologically coherent raw-p patterns — most notably the E6C10/Th1
Pos-vs-Neg axis in Comp4. This reflects the test budget (250-300 tests
per comparison) rather than the absence of an effect. Raw-p signals
appearing at consistent magnitudes across stimulations are
hypothesis-generating but require independent validation.

The Prolif-vs-NonProlif analysis (588 tests, 60% FDR-significant) is
not a screen. The two cell fractions are biologically distinct by
definition, so the relevant inferential output is the direction pattern
across readouts, not per-readout significance.

Cross-sectional design
----------------------
Comp2, Comp3, and Comp4 are cross-sectional comparisons across IGRA
status or disease state. They do not support causal inference about
maternal-to-infant immune transfer. Comp1 (paired longitudinal) is the
only design in this script that supports inference about temporal
change within individuals.

Imbalance flagging
------------------
The implemented note_imbalance flag in Comp3 (triggering when n_pos < 5)
did not fire for the 9 vs 25 imbalance present in this dataset. A
softer threshold (e.g. min(n_pos, n_neg) < 10, or a ratio criterion
exceeding 2:1) would have flagged it.

IGRA semantics
--------------
Maternal IGRA "INT" (intermediate) values are treated as positive
upstream of these analyses; "ATB" (active TB) is treated as a separate
clinical category and not pooled with positive. Where a single PID
carries inconsistent IGRA labels across rows, the highest-priority
label (ATB > Pos > Neg > INT) is retained. Comparisons reported here
reflect these definitions.
